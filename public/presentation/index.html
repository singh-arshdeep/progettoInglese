How to talk about a device

Linkers of manner: how something happens

LinkerMeaningExample from our review
asin the same way thatIt works exactly as you expect.
as iflike (but it's not real)Apps open as if they were already running.
as though= as if, more formalThe screen looks as though it were made of glass.
the wayhow, the manner in whichI love the way Apple Intelligence helps me.

Watch out: as or as if?

as = real

It works as you expect.

It really works like that.

as if / as though = not real

Apps open as if they were already running.

They aren't running, it only seems so.

Tip: after as if we often use the past: were, not "are".

Recommending phrases

  1. I would definitely recommend it to + person

    I would definitely recommend it to anyone who wants a cutting-edge phone.

  2. My advice is to + verb

    My advice is to buy a strong case to protect it.

We use them at the end of a review, in the opinion and recommendation part.
